# Vision anchor photos

Small example photos the local classifier compares a garment photo against
when two labels look alike in text space (see clothing-vision/encoder.ts).

- `t-shirt/`: product photos sampled from the public "Fashion Product Images
  (Small)" dataset (Kaggle/Myntra, labelled Public Domain),
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/paramaggarwal/fashion-product-images-small
- `polo-shirt/`: photos from Wikimedia Commons —
  "Fumarel piqué polo shirt" (CC0) and Uniqlo polo shirt photos by
  BrokenSphere / Wikimedia Commons contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- `dress-shoes/` (heels and formal shoes), `sandals/`, `flip-flops/`, `flats/`:
  product photos sampled from the same Fashion Product Images (Small)
  dataset (Public Domain), used to tell the four shoe types apart.
